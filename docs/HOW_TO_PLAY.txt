CONTINGENCY ZERO
================

Starting the game
-----------------
Windows: unzip the folder anywhere and double-click ContingencyZero.exe.
  (Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".)
Linux:   extract the archive and run ./ContingencyZero.x86_64

Your first match
----------------
1. Main menu > Skirmish. Pick a map (Proving Grounds is small and quick),
   your faction, and one computer opponent on Easy. Press Start.
2. Your Commander is selected with the Home key. Its build menu is on the right.
3. Build Metal Extractors on the dark patches of metal ore near your base,
   then Solar Collectors (or Wind Turbines on windy maps) for energy,
   then a Bot Lab or Vehicle Plant.
4. Select the factory and left-click units to queue them (shift-click adds 5).
5. Drag a box around your army, then Ctrl+right-click (attack-move) toward the enemy.
6. Destroy the enemy Commander to win. Keep yours alive: lose it and you lose.

Economy
-------
Metal and energy flow continuously. Everything under construction draws from
the flow; if income can't keep up, all building slows down evenly (a "stall").
Watch the two bars at the top. Wrecks, rocks and trees can be reclaimed by
builders (R) for metal and energy.

Controls
--------
Camera     Arrow keys or screen edges scroll, mouse wheel zooms,
           middle-drag pans, Q / E turn the view, click the minimap to jump.
Select     Left click, or drag a box. Shift adds. Double-click selects all of
           that type on screen. Ctrl+A all combat units, Ctrl+B all builders,
           Home your Commander. Ctrl+1..9 saves a group, 1..9 recalls it.
Orders     Right click: move / attack / help build / reclaim / set rally point.
           Shift+right click queues orders. Ctrl+right click attack-moves.
           A attack, S stop, M move, P patrol, G guard, R reclaim,
           D Breach Cannon (Commander), F fire state, Tab health bars.
Building   Pick a structure, click to place. Drag to place a row,
           hold shift to keep placing. Right click cancels.
Game       + / - game speed (single player), Pause pauses, Esc menu.

The in-game Controls screen (main menu or Esc menu) has the full list.

Controller (Steam Deck, Xbox, PlayStation pads)
-----------------------------------------------
Left stick moves a cursor, right stick pans, LT / RT zoom. A selects (hold A
and move for a box), X orders at the cursor, Y opens the order wheel, B
cancels or deselects. With a builder or factory selected the d-pad walks its
build menu; A picks, then A places. LB taps through control groups (hold it to
add or queue), RB selects your army, then builders. View finds your Commander
(hold it to steer on the minimap). Menu pauses. In menus the d-pad moves,
A presses and B goes back. On a Steam Deck use the Gamepad controller layout;
Settings has a Steam Deck preset (bigger interface, button prompts always on).

Modes
-----
Skirmish     You against up to 7 computer players (Easy, Normal, Hard, Brutal).
Campaign     Two short campaigns, one per faction.
Multiplayer  Host or join over a LAN or by IP address (UDP port 7777).
             Internet play needs that port forwarded on the host's router.
Map Editor   Make your own maps; they appear in the Skirmish map list.
Replays      Every match can be saved and watched again.

Your settings, maps, replays and campaign progress are kept in
  Windows: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Contingency Zero\
  Linux:   ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Contingency Zero/
